ARPHA11

ARPHA11 is an independent software company founded and operated by Judah Adonija Bolagh, a young software developer with a simple goal: build software, experiment with technology, and make money doing it.

ARPHA11 exists to create software without being locked into a single philosophy. Some projects are built to be completely free and open source. Others are developed as proprietary, closed-source products. Both approaches have a place — developers should be free to choose the model that fits a given project.

At its core, ARPHA11 is about building things — a small command-line utility, a developer tool, an operating-system component, a game, a programming language, a productivity application, or a larger commercial product. It is meant to be a place where ideas become working software.

The ARPHAXAD Structure

ARPHA11 operates under a broader project structure known as ARPHAXAD, divided into two primary branches.

AXD

ArphaXaD — proprietary & commercial

Closed-source, commercially licensed, paid, or otherwise restricted products — turning development work into sustainable revenue.

"Build useful software. Make it good. Sell it." Commercial applications, utilities, games, professional software, and specialized tools, held to the same standard of functionality and quality as the rest of ARPHA11.

OPS

OpenProjectSource — free & open source

Software developed to be inspected, used, modified, learned from, and contributed to, depending on the individual project's license.

A space to experiment publicly, share knowledge, and build tools that can evolve beyond their original creator — not every OPS project needs to become a major product.

A One-Person Software Company

Unlike a large technology corporation, ARPHA11 is operated by a single developer: Judah Adonija Bolagh — interested in creating software across different areas of computing. There is no engineering department, corporate hierarchy, or development team behind it.

  • One person designs it.
  • One person writes it.
  • One person maintains it.
  • One person decides what gets built next.

Being a one-person company means ARPHA11 can experiment with ideas that might never survive inside a conventional corporate environment — started because they're interesting, changed when something better is discovered, abandoned when they stop being useful.

The company is intentionally small. The ambition, however, does not have to be.

The ARPHA11 Philosophy

Build software. Not every idea needs to become a billion-dollar startup — sometimes the best reason to create something is that it would be useful, interesting, challenging, or fun.
Learn by building. Every project is a chance to learn something new about programming, operating systems, interfaces, architecture, networking, or whatever the project requires.
Open source when it makes sense — and proprietary when it makes sense. Open source and proprietary software are tools, not religions.
Make money. If a project is valuable enough for people to pay for, ARPHA11 should be able to sell it — funding future projects and letting Judah spend more time doing what he actually wants to do: make software.
